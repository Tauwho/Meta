# Research: making money on Medium (as of 2026-11-20)

## 1. How Medium pays writers

- **Medium Partner Program (MPP)** is the only first-party income source. Writers are paid from a pool funded by paying-member subscriptions.
- Earnings are driven mainly by **member reading time** on paywalled stories, plus engagement signals (claps, highlights, replies, follows). Medium also pays when a non-member hits your paywall and subscribes to keep reading.
- A member read is commonly reported to count only after ~30 seconds of reading (third-party claim; Medium publishes no per-read rate).
- **Eligibility:** be a paying Medium member, 18+, have published at least one story in the last 6 months, and hold a bank account + tax residency in a supported country. Payouts go through Stripe. Medium expanded MPP to 77 more countries in Aug 2024 (~119 total). **Check Singapore on the official supported-countries page before enrolling** (could not be verified automatically; Medium's help center blocks automated fetches).
- Realistic numbers: most new writers report **$5–$50/month**; earnings are much lower for non-English content.

## 2. Distribution (how stories get read)

- Every story is eligible for **general distribution** via the recommendation algorithm (Explore, "Recommended for you", feeds), with spam/NSFW filtering.
- **Boost**: human curators (publication editors in the Boost Nomination Program plus Medium's internal team) nominate stories for extra distribution. Boosted stories get much more reach. Criteria: constructive, original, written from relevant experience, well-crafted, memorable. Length does not matter; only recent stories qualify.
- **Publications** matter: submitting to large publications (especially ones in the Boost Nomination Program) is the main lever for reach on a new account.
- Two-thirds of Medium stories are reported as not paywalled (Feb 2026, third-party report of CEO statement).

## 3. AI rules — the constraint that shapes this whole project

- Since **1 May 2024**: AI-generated writing, **disclosed or not, may not be paywalled** in the Partner Program.
- Scope (help center): stories where "the majority of the content has been generated by an AI writing tool, with little or no human editing, fact-checking, or original contribution". AI-assisted outlining, spell/grammar checking and fact verification are explicitly fine.
- AI-generated writing is **not eligible for distribution or Boost**.
- Penalties: removal from the paywall, removal of stories, **revocation of Partner Program enrollment**.
- Detection is imperfect (critics note detector false positives), but curation is human, and Medium signals it is doubling down on human curation as its AI defence.

**Conclusion:** a zero-human pipeline cannot legitimately earn MPP money and would mostly be invisible (no distribution). The chosen design is **AI-centered with one real human gate per story**: AI does research, briefing, drafting, fact-checking, editing, SEO, packaging, scheduling and analytics; the human adds genuine original contribution (experience, opinion, verification) and publishes. See `POLICY.md`.

## 4. Automation surface

- **Official API is dead for new users.** `github.com/Medium/medium-api-docs` was archived on 2 Mar 2023 and says the API "is no longer supported" and Medium does not allow new integrations. Old self-issued integration tokens may still work for accounts that already have one; new tokens are not issued (one source dates the cutoff to 1 Jan 2025).
- Workarounds seen in the wild: Playwright/browser automation using a logged-in session (several MCP servers do this). This is fragile and risks Medium's terms on automated access. **Not used here**: the human gate already includes the publish click, so no automation of Medium's site is needed.
- **Read-only signals that are safe to automate:** public RSS feeds (`https://medium.com/feed/@handle`, `https://medium.com/feed/tag/<tag>`, `https://medium.com/feed/<publication>`). Used for trend scouting and publish confirmation.
- **Earnings data:** no API. The human downloads/copies stats into `data/earnings/` occasionally; the AI ingests them.

## 5. Strategy implications

1. Pick 1–2 niches where the human has real experience (e.g. CS student life in Singapore, SWE internship hunting, payments engineering, OS/DSA explainers). Experience-led writing is what Boost rewards and what keeps stories paywall-eligible.
2. Cadence: 2–3 stories/week is sustainable with AI doing ~80% of the work.
3. Submit every story to a fitting publication (AI drafts the submission pitch).
4. Feedback loop: track which topics/titles earn reading time and feed that back into idea scoring.
5. Optional off-platform upside: canonical-free cross-posting is allowed, newsletter capture via Medium's built-in email subscriptions, affiliate links only with disclosure.
